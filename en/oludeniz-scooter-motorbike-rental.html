About This Experience

The easiest way around narrow bay roads and along the coast. We deliver scooters and motorbikes with helmets to your hotel — ride freely between Ölüdeniz, Hisarönü and Fethiye with no parking hassle.

Included

  • Helmets (rider & passenger)
  • Third-party insurance
  • Hotel delivery & pickup
  • Roadside assistance

Not Included

  • Fuel
  • Traffic fines
  • Damage beyond the excess

Important Information

  • 50cc requires a B or A1 licence; 125cc+ requires an A1/A2/A licence
  • Rider at least 18
  • Daily and weekly rental
  • Price depends on model and duration — get a quote on WhatsApp

What to Bring

  • Driving licence

Frequently Asked Questions

Which licence do I need?

A B or A1 licence is enough for a 50cc scooter. Motorbikes of 125cc and above require an A1, A2 or A licence.

Do you provide helmets?

Yes, helmets for rider and passenger are free — wearing one is required by law.
