About This Experience

The classic boat trip from Ölüdeniz: Butterfly Valley, the Blue Cave, St. Nicholas (Gemiler) Island, Camel Beach and Cold Water Bay. Swimming stops all day, lunch on board and the best views of the Lycian coast.

Included

  • Hotel transfer (Ölüdeniz, Hisarönü, Ovacık)
  • Insurance
  • Swimming stops

Not Included

  • Drinks
  • Island / valley entrance fees
  • Photos and video

Important Information

  • Departure: Ölüdeniz beach pier
  • The captain may change the route depending on weather and sea
  • Suitable for families and children
  • Message us on WhatsApp for price and daily availability

What to Bring

  • Swimsuit and towel
  • Sunscreen
  • Hat
  • Cash (for entrance fees)

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do we stay at Butterfly Valley?

The boat stops at the valley for about an hour; you can take a short walk to the waterfall.

What if I get seasick?

The Ölüdeniz bays are usually calm. If you are sensitive, take seasickness medicine before the trip.
