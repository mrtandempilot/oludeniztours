Frequently Asked Questions

What should I bring?

A swimsuit/shorts that can get wet, water shoes that stay on your feet (rentable if needed) and — most importantly — dry spare clothes for after.

Do I pay extra for transfer and meals?

No, it's all inclusive! Hotel transfer, a village breakfast by the river and lunch at the end of the tour are included.

Age limit and participation conditions?

Due to the exciting rapids, the minimum age is 13. Anyone without serious health issues can join.

Do I have to know how to swim?

Professional life jackets keep you afloat with no extra effort. Just follow your guide's instructions.

Am I safe?

Absolutely. Every raft has an internationally trained river guide; helmets and life jackets keep safety at the highest level.

Where does the tour take place?

On the Dalaman River, one of Türkiye's most famous courses, about 1.5 hours from Fethiye.
