Frequently Asked Questions

Is the boat private to us?

Yes — the boat and captain are exclusively yours, no other guests. Route and breaks are planned around your group.

Which routes can I choose from?

Two options: the Darboğaz route or the Butterfly Valley route. Just tell us your preference on WhatsApp when booking.

How many people can join?

Boat capacity is limited; message us your group size on WhatsApp and we'll plan the best option.

Are there swimming breaks?

Yes — swimming and photo breaks at blue-flag bays. Don't forget swimsuit, towel and sunscreen.

Can we bring children?

Yes, suitable for all ages. Life jackets are available for kids; a calm route is recommended for families with babies.

Are food and drinks included?

No — food and drinks are not included. You may bring your own or buy at the beach before departure.
