Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the tour?

Starts around 08:30-09:00 and ends around 18:00-18:30 — a full-day adventure.

Which places are visited?

The classic Fethiye route includes Tlos Ancient City, Yakapark, Saklıkent Canyon, Gizlikent Waterfall and the mud bath.

Is it safe?

Yes — vehicles get periodic maintenance and drivers are experienced on these routes. Stay seated and hold on for safety.

Is it suitable for children?

Yes, kids love it. The bumps can be tiring for very small babies; ideal for ages 6+.

What should I wear?

Quick-dry shorts and t-shirts are best. Pick sandals or water shoes that stay on; wear your swimsuit underneath.

Is lunch included?

Yes — usually grilled trout, chicken or vegetarian options; drinks are extra.

Are photos and videos taken?

Yes, a professional team shoots during the tour. You can buy the footage at the end for an extra fee.

Can I do another activity the same day?

Not recommended — the tour takes about 10 hours and is physically tiring. Plan something calm for the evening.
