Ölüdeniz Paragliding Guide: Price, Duration, Safety

2026-11-05 · Oludeniz Tours

Tandem paragliding from Babadağ down to Ölüdeniz is one of the most famous flying spots in the world. Here are answers to the questions first-timers ask most.

What is it like?

You're picked up from your hotel and driven to the summit of Babadağ (around 1,965 m). Your pilot gives a short safety briefing, you run a few steps and the wing gently lifts you up. There's no falling sensation — it feels like gliding. You land on Ölüdeniz beach.

How long does it take?

Time in the air is 25-30 minutes on average, depending on the weather. Including pickup, the drive up and return, allow about 1.5-2 hours in total.

How much does it cost?

The current price is 6,500 ₺ per person including hotel transfer, insurance and GoPro photos/video. A 1,000 ₺ deposit secures your booking and the rest is paid on the day. Options like a sunset flight or a female pilot cost extra. Full details: Paragliding.

Are there age and weight limits?

  • The minimum age is usually 4; the upper limit depends on health and the pilot's approval.
  • Standard weight limits are 80 kg for women and 100 kg for men; a surcharge applies above that.
  • Not recommended for people with heart conditions or panic attacks.

What should I wear?

Trainers that won't come off and sunglasses are enough. The summit can be cooler than the beach, so a light windbreaker is a good idea. Flip-flops are not allowed.

What if the weather is bad?

The pilots decide every morning based on real conditions. If it's not suitable, your flight is moved to another day or refunded.

What's the best time to fly?

Morning flights usually have calmer air; sunset flights offer the most stunning colours. Book early in summer.
