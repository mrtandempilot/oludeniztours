Frequently Asked Questions

Does health condition matter?

If you have a condition (herniated disc, pregnancy, etc.), let us know before the tour.

Is there a speed limit?

Yes — this is a convoy tour, not a race. Follow the guide's set speed and spacing.

Do I have to wear goggles?

Definitely! Wear sunglasses or the protective goggles provided against dust and small stones.

Can children ride?

Kids over 12-14 can ride as passengers behind a parent. Driving solo requires age 16+.

Is the tour cancelled in rain?

On the contrary — rain doubles the fun! More mud, more adrenaline. The tour continues unless safety is at risk.

Can I bring a bag?

Big backpacks affect balance; a small waist bag is ideal. Valuables can be left in lockers at the office.

Will I get dirty?

"Very" is an understatement! Dust, dirt and puddles everywhere — wear your oldest clothes.

No driving license — can I drive?

Yes! Tours run on closed forest and off-road tracks, so no license is needed. A short training is enough.
