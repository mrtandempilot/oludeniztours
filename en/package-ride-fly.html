Frequently Asked Questions

What if I'm late for the tour?

Transfers and departure times run punctually for organized groups. If delayed, contact us immediately on WhatsApp.

Can I film with my own phone/camera?

Our professional team shoots throughout the tour; you can buy the footage digitally at the end for an extra fee.

What should I wear on tour day?

Sport pants or leggings and closed sport shoes are mandatory. Flip-flops, sandals, shorts or skirts are not allowed for safety. Sunglasses and sunscreen recommended.

I've never ridden a horse — can I join?

Yes. No riding experience needed; our instructors brief you on horse handling and safety before the tour. Our horses are gentle and specially trained.

Who is responsible if I lose my belongings?

Personal items forgotten or lost during the tour are the participant's responsibility. Inform your guide immediately if something is lost.

Weight, age or health limits?

For the horse tour: max weight 100-110 kg, minimum age 6. Pregnant guests and those with serious disc or joint conditions cannot join for safety.

Is there insurance coverage?

Yes. The whole process — from transfer to activities — is covered by seat and third-party liability insurance.

What's included? Any extras?

Round-trip transfer, professional guiding, helmet and protective equipment, entrance fees and mandatory travel insurance are included. Photos/video are extra. No surprise fees.

What are my rights if I cancel?

Cancellations made at least 48 hours before departure get a full refund. No refund within the last 48 hours.

What if weather prevents the tour?

If we cancel for safety reasons, you get a full refund or we reschedule to a date of your choice.
