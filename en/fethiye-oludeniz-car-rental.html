About This Experience

Explore the region at your own pace. From economy cars to SUVs and 7-9 seat vans; we deliver to your hotel or Dalaman Airport. Kayaköy, Saklıkent, Butterfly Valley and the Lycian bays are all within reach.

Included

  • Comprehensive & third-party insurance
  • Unlimited mileage (daily rentals)
  • Hotel delivery & pickup (Ölüdeniz, Hisarönü, Ovacık, Fethiye)
  • 24/7 roadside assistance

Not Included

  • Fuel
  • Toll fees
  • Traffic fines

Important Information

  • Driver at least 21, licence held 2+ years
  • Passport / ID and valid driving licence required
  • Automatic and manual options
  • Airport delivery may cost extra
  • Price depends on season and car class — get a quote on WhatsApp

What to Bring

  • Driving licence
  • Passport / ID
  • Credit card (for deposit)

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I pick the car up at the airport?

Yes, pickup and return at Dalaman Airport is possible. Just send us your flight details on WhatsApp.

Do you take a deposit?

Depending on the car class, a credit-card hold or cash deposit is taken and returned when the car comes back without issues.

Can I add an extra driver?

Yes, just provide the extra driver's licence and ID at handover.
